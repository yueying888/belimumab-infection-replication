# Data access and boundaries

- FAERS: obtain public quarterly files from the US FDA.
- JADER: obtain public data from PMDA and comply with its conditions of use, including publication notification.
- MedDRA: obtain under the applicable license; terminology files are not redistributed.
- Local clinical data: not public because participant privacy and institutional governance apply. Requests require corresponding-author and institutional review.
- Included aggregate CSV files are sufficient to inspect the published tables and figure-source records, but not to reconstruct upstream report selection.
